Pother Bondhu: an animal rescue network for Dhaka

See a street animal that needs help? Post a photo with the area. Volunteers in that area see it and go to help.

Report an animal or join as a volunteer.

What you can do here

  • Rescue board: photos of street animals in trouble, filtered by area or shown on a map of Dhaka, each marked Urgent, Needs vet, Being helped or Resolved.
  • Adoption: animals volunteers have looked after that now need a home.
  • Vets and rescue groups: vet hospitals, rescue groups and shelters in Dhaka, with the source of each detail.
  • Teams: volunteer teams for feeding routes, night rescue and vaccination camps.
  • Volunteers: a public list of volunteers by area, who you can support with PetPoints.
  • Supplies shop: food, bowls and beds for street animals, paid in PetPoints.

Made by Faiaz Abrar Zaman Pronoy and Sinthia Khandoker. Available in Bangla and English.
